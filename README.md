A custom-built desktop hardware companion that tracks your study sessions and monitors your room's environment.

View the project on the [Half Life page](https://halflife.hackclub.com/projects/a25muen80i5c7zvo2wi1umca).

## What it is
ClimaFader merges a Pomodoro focus timer with real-time climate monitoring. You use a physical slide potentiometer to set the focus or break intervals, and tactile buttons to start, pause, or switch modes. The OLED screen displays your remaining time, accumulated "XP" (which are the completed sessions), and the current room temperature and humidity. When a session ends, a buzzer sounds an alert.
